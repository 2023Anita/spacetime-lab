## 录制说明 / Recording notes

The video is a 1920×1080, 30 fps H.264 MP4, approximately 64 seconds, without an audio track. Six experiments receive nine seconds each, with a four-second opening and a six-second end card. Recording mode simplifies the layout. Original frame timestamps preserve the interaction timing before conversion to constant frame rate. Raw recordings and temporary assembly files remain local.
